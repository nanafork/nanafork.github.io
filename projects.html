Adaptive Drift-Aware Credit Card Fraud Detection Framework

LSTM & Swarm Intelligence

Developed a drift-aware Credit Card Fraud (CCF) detection framework leveraging an online Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) architecture integrated with ADWIN and EDDM drift monitors. Engineered a Memory-Based Cuckoo Search (MCS) metaheuristic algorithm for high-dimensional feature selection and integrated MAHAKIL chromosomal-oversampling to conquer extreme class imbalance (>99% accuracy across European cardholder benchmark datasets).

Online LSTM + ADWIN/EDDM MAHAKIL Oversampling Memory Cuckoo Search

ViT COVID-19 & SARS Detection Application

Vision Transformers & Healthcare

Mentored and collaborated with undergraduate researchers on computer vision architectures applying Bootstrapped Vision Transformers (ViT-B16) to chest CT-scan slices, facilitating accurate and early identification of pulmonary SARS and COVID-19 lesions. Published in Springer's Iran Journal of Computer Science (2025).

Malware Detection via Resampled Correlation Descriptors & LSTM

Deep Learning & Cybersecurity
