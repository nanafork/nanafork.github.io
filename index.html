About Me

I am a Machine Learning Researcher at the Data Intelligence and Swarm Analytics Laboratory (DISAL) in the Department of Computer Science, University of Ghana, and Founder at Cortex Consult. My core research interests span Adaptive Machine Learning, Concept Drift Detection, Swarm Intelligence / Metaheuristic Optimization, and Computer Vision.

I have mentored undergraduate students conducting machine learning research applied to real-world challenges in public health diagnostics, financial cybersecurity, and agriculture.

PhD Candidacy: I am actively seeking and exploring PhD positions for Fall 2027 in Computer Science and Machine Learning. If your lab is working on adaptive learning, streaming data drift detection, or vision transformers, please feel free to reach out!

Selected Projects

Adaptive Drift-Aware Credit Card Fraud Detection Framework

LSTM & Swarm Intelligence

Developed a drift-aware Credit Card Fraud (CCF) detection framework leveraging an online Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) architecture integrated with ADWIN and EDDM drift monitors. Engineered a Memory-Based Cuckoo Search (MCS) metaheuristic algorithm for high-dimensional feature selection and integrated MAHAKIL chromosomal-oversampling to conquer extreme class imbalance (>99% accuracy across European cardholder benchmark datasets).

ViT COVID-19 & SARS Detection Application

Vision Transformers & Healthcare

Mentored and collaborated with undergraduate researchers on computer vision architectures applying Bootstrapped Vision Transformers (ViT-B16) to chest CT-scan slices, facilitating accurate and early identification of pulmonary SARS and COVID-19 lesions.

Malware Detection via Resampled Correlation Descriptors & LSTM

Deep Learning & Cybersecurity

Constructed a recurrent deep learning pipeline utilizing resampled correlation-based feature descriptors to capture subtle behavioral anomalies in mobile malware, achieving 98% classification accuracy over benchmark threat repositories.
